How to Play

  1. Scan. Sweep your phone slowly across the room. Floors, counters and beds light up.
  2. Mark cover. Tap the glowing reticle on a kitchen island, the end of a bed, a couch — anywhere a wabbit could duck behind.
  3. Hunt. He pops up from behind your real furniture. Hold anywhere to shoulder the gun and aim down the barrel.
  4. Miss. Release to fire. He will dodge, catch the pellets in his teeth, or bend your barrel into a pretzel.
  5. Score style. You can't hit him, so you're scored on how spectacularly you fail.
